# All-Run Synthesis And Hypothesis Update - 2026-09-04

> Later interpretation: the [September 28 reassessment](measurement_reassessment_2026_09_28.md)
> adds the human-origin pilot and a success/failure text audit. In particular,
> five paired contract recoveries must not be read as five identical binding
> failures. This historical synthesis and its frozen scores are retained.

## Position

The user's September 4 reframing changes the primary forward question:
**If a system gains expressive ability, can that expand what it can reason
about, discover, or make usable for another reasoner?** Reliable transmission
alone cannot answer this. The sender and receiver may compensate for an
underspecified artifact through shared assumptions or independent recomputation.
This possibility is not evidence of intentional collusion, and it has not been
isolated as the cause of the current successes.

The strongest accumulated result is not that language expression is generally
weak. It is that **usable distinction preservation depends on the whole
communication contract**, and that several apparent semantic losses shrink or
disappear when that contract is calibrated. Other losses remain after literal
extraction succeeds. These are different observations, not competing slogans.

The September 3 ceiling is a successful local calibration and a useful negative
result for an intrinsic ordinary-prose deficit on that surface. It is not the
culmination of a demonstrated general intelligence bottleneck. We should spend
the next research effort explaining the accumulated contrasts before launching
the previously proposed 4,212-call successor. No new model calls were made for
this synthesis, and that successor has not been started.

This is a retrospective analysis, not a new preregistration. It supersedes the
*current-status reading* of the older working map, without replacing historical
notes, protocols, scores, or failed gates. The non-reduction clause remains:
expression can create distinctions, reorganize its author, change a reader,
and make new problems possible. Rule-Z measures none of those exhaustively.

## What Survives Reinterpretation

### 1. Case Binding Matters; Self-Contracts Are Not Uniquely Necessary

The early Claude sender study gives 23/30 free transmissions versus 30/30
factlocked transmissions with D/O at 30/30. The contract study gives 25/30
schema-framed transmissions versus 30/30 under self-contract, oracle contract,
case hint without sections, factlock, and oracle text. The five contrast
packets contain concrete omissions: a general predicate vocabulary is sent
where the actual case facts were needed.
Those five packets were selected on failure plus recovery outcomes. They are
explanatory examples, not an unbiased sample for estimating the binding effect.

But "free" asked the model to describe the **rule system**. This is not an
unambiguous instruction to transmit every case distinction. The case-hint
control is central: ordinary prose can work without a typed template. The
newly recovered ladder confirms Claude 26/30 -> 30/30 with a case hint and
30/30 without sections. The mini model reaches 27/30 with the hint, 22/30
without sections, and 29/30 factlocked. These are prompt/model-local contrasts,
not proof that one provider possesses and another lacks expression ability.

"Pure transmission loss" remains a useful **operational selection**:
`D correct AND O correct AND T wrong`. Separate successful D/O calls do not
prove that the failed sender call contained an identical, correct latent state.
Successful alternate messages also do not prove the receiver is faultless on
the failed message. Paired case evidence localizes a pathway, not one hidden
causal component with certainty.

### 2. Contracts Steer Behavior, But Steering Is Not Yet Capacity Growth

The recovered 390-row perturbation study is particularly useful because it
includes a negative intervention:

| Claude, same 30 cases | Correct |
| --- | ---: |
| Free schema | 29/30 |
| Self, oracle, generic, contract-only, factlocked, oracle text | 30/30 each |
| Case hint without sections | 29/30 |
| Scrambled contract | 24/30 |
| Wrong contract | 7/30 |

The contract can influence the later prose; it is not necessarily ignored.
However, `wrong_contract` explicitly supplies false case facts and requests
single-conclusion collapse, while `scrambled_contract` exchanges semantic
roles. They are multi-component misinformation/instruction interventions,
not isolated perturbations of a hidden binding variable. Their labels are also
visible in the prompt. We cannot assign the whole decrement to one mechanism.

The self-contract prompt itself specifies four preservation requirements.
Thus "self-generated" means producing case-specific content under supplied
axes, not autonomously discovering the communication objective from nothing.
`contract_only` removes the original Z from the prose-generation call and
succeeds at 30/30 here and 48/48 on the stress run. That establishes usefulness
for the original endpoint. Contracts can already contain the derived active
conclusion, so this is not proof of complete, reusable world compression.

### 3. Repair Is Promising, But The Same-Draft Causal Contrast Is Missing

The recovered repair study has free 27/30 and repair 30/30. A new raw-metadata
check finds **0/30 repair drafts are identical to the separately scored free
messages**. The repair condition generates a fresh draft, then revises it with
access to structured Z; those initial drafts were not separately receiver-scored.

Therefore revise "it repaired all three failed messages" to "the repair
pipeline succeeds on the three cases where the separate free pipeline fails."
Same-case recovery is real; the contribution of critique versus a fresh sample,
source re-access, and another call is not isolated. This does not invalidate
repairability, but prevents an attractive result from becoming an unsupported
process-level claim.

### 4. The Strongest Binding Evidence Is The Equal-Call Factorial

On Claude's 12-case high-load subset, the two-call free derivation path reaches
19/24 while the two-call generic-contract path reaches 24/24. The later 2x2
cross gives compact/free 19/24 and each of explicit/free, compact/generic,
explicit/generic 24/24. Compact/free replicate answer agreement is 9/12;
controlled cells are 12/12.

This weakens "just another call" as an explanation. It does not equalize actual
tokens, internal computation, source access, or instruction content. At ceiling,
the zero extra benefit from combining notation and contract does not establish
mechanistic equivalence or antagonism.

Stored derivations locate some failures at priority orientation, others after
correct local edges during global integration. A wrong state can also produce
the correct `conflict` label. This is stronger evidence than the initial eta:
eta ranges below zero and above one and depends on a no-information receiver
prior. It is not physical channel capacity or a source-information fraction.

### 5. Reader Behavior Is Not An Information Meter

Both completed readers use exactly the same 96 frozen source artifacts.
Compact/free current utility is 0.845 for Claude and 0.839 for GPT-5.5;
the other source cells reach 1.000 for both. This is a useful cross-reader
convergence on a local source-condition boundary, not a fresh sender replication.

In contrast, faithful full-state scores are 0/96 and 27/96, while repair
full-state scores are 90/96 and 92/96. Target-cued counterfactual utility differs
much more: 0.591 versus 0.990. These utilities are averages over field/battery
scores, not interchangeable binary trial-success rates.

A reader's failure cannot establish that the source lacks information when
another reader succeeds; the latter may recompute. Nor does success prove
explicit source fidelity. The controlled Luna audit moves calibrated faithful
performance from 31/120 to 76/120 by changing its ontology rubric, yet valid
contradiction sensitivity remains 0.500. The measurement instrument has its
own false positives, false negatives, grounding obligations, and repair policy.

### 6. Extraction Is Neither Sufficient Nor Always Necessary For The Endpoint

The extraction/intervention studies keep source support separate from hidden
world truth. All-field exact model literals still precede downstream failure:

| Study | Exact upstream rows | Full downstream failures among them |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Four-world pilot | 24 | 4, all one omitted-dependency case |
| Sixteen-world scale-up | 144 | 29 across 12 artifact cases / 9 worlds |
| Same worlds, target versus null | 124 | 22 |

These are **localizations**, not causal effect estimates conditioned on an
untreated covariate: cue assignment itself changes which ledgers are exact.
They also combine different downstream defects. In the scale-up, 14 of 15
counterfactual-complete exact-upstream failures are support-only failures;
14 omitted-dependency failures fail the answer/active-state requirement.

The distinction `unresolved conclusions != contradictory source definitions`
is a recurring boundary. Endpoint accuracy can hide wrong support judgments;
a private-world guess from an insufficient source is not successful transmission.
Conversely, value-inexact extraction can still support the queried endpoint
because an omitted item may be irrelevant, recomputed, or masked by label
coarseness. This does not make fidelity irrelevant.

Direct prose and oracle-normalized ledgers do not have a universal ordering:
58/64 versus 54/64 in the pilot, 207/256 versus 206/256 in the scale-up, with
different family-specific directions. The model-literal path is lower overall
(44/64, then 166/256), especially under contradictory dependencies. Eight
independent field calls are not automatically a jointly coherent representation.

### 7. Binding Is Directional And Can Compete With Itself

Against uncued input, target announcement improves active-conclusion fidelity
but reduces active-rule fidelity in both pilot and scale-up. Against the
length-matched formatting instruction, it improves both. The latter comparison
has nine strictly improving targets, one tie (`current_answer`), and one
regression (`rule_definitions`) among eleven targets. This corrects the prior
note's "ten of eleven" wording; its table is unchanged.

The null is an imperative task, not inert padding. The repeated target arm is
broadly stable across runs; the new null is descriptively much worse than the
old uncued arm on several fields. Because those arms are noncontemporaneous,
the difference does not identify a pure null cost. Still, the pattern makes
"extra words improve capacity" and "binding uniformly improves fidelity"
inadequate accounts.

The supported statement is comparative: aligned task instructions outperform
that competing instruction on most measured targets. A specific attention
budget or internal allocation mechanism remains an interpretation, not an
observed variable.

### 8. Prompt-Local Old-Rule Leakage Was Not The Dominant Failure

The 288-case revision study observes strict sender leakage 0/576 in each of
delta and full-restatement conditions. Receiver-only strict leakage under
exact input is also zero. The typed interface is a substantial negative result
for the predicted stale-rule effect under this contract. Do not turn it into
positive leakage evidence by renaming every packet error.

Delta packets are exact in 439/576 versus 571/576 after full restatement,
mostly because of history serialization. A separate post-hoc diagnostic finds
delta history role completeness 496/576 and content coverage 560/576, without
promoting role-unidentified content to semantic success. Load 8/16/32 is not a
monotonic failure curve. Much of that load is inactive distractor rules; it is
not dependency depth or a growing chain of reasoning.

These are single-prompt version changes, not memory persistence, sequential
conversation revisions, weight editing, or unlearning. A zero strict compound
event does not estimate all possible stale-rule influence. The current-state
and old-atom checks are necessary companion metrics.

### 9. Reinterpret The Adverse Strong-Cue Result At Field Level

The subsequent 108-case interface study has typed strong joint 103/216 versus
neutral 132/216. That observed reversal must remain in the ledger. But a new
[field-level reanalysis](../assets/analyses/all_run_synthesis_2026_09_04/focused_reanalysis.json)
locates it more precisely:

| Joint condition, n=216 each | Strong | Neutral | Full restatement |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Inexact derivation | 112 | 83 | 118 |
| Inexact fired rules | 5 | 0 | 5 |
| Inexact fired priority edges | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Inexact suppressed rules | 106 | 70 | 114 |
| Inexact active rules | 51 | 57 | 72 |
| Inexact active conclusions | 3 | 0 | 0 |
| Nonempty suppression despite no fired priority edge | 88 | 55 | 99 |
| Active rules equal the entire current catalog, incorrectly | 29 | 42 | 53 |

The fields overlap, so their counts must not be added. `suppressed_rules`
often contains a retired/superseded historical rule; `active_rules` can mean
the valid current catalog instead of **fired minus suppressed**. In the 18
rows per condition with a priority edge between same-conclusion fired rules,
empty suppression occurs 18, 15, and 15 times. The oracle suppresses the loser
even when its conclusion agrees with the winner.

The sender prompt lists field names and empty lists but does not fully define
these distinctions or item schemas. This makes **field-ontology binding** a
concrete competing explanation for the low exact score. These remain failures
under the frozen task definition; they are not silently forgiven. The new
counts are post-hoc patterns, not proof of why the model chose those meanings.

The adverse cue effect therefore does not yet prove a general cost of stronger
binding to expression capacity. It does disprove treating that intervention
as a universally beneficial scalar. The next contract must distinguish
historical retirement, failure to fire, priority suppression, and active
derivation membership before calling residual errors loss of intelligence.

Answer-preserving revisions are a useful stressor precisely because the label
cannot reveal whether the revised structure was preserved. Strong typed joint
exactness is 84/144 on changing revisions and 19/72 on preserving revisions,
despite complete revision-uptake checks. That gap is real under the scorer, but
the two case classes have different constructions, including same-conclusion
priorities. It is not an isolated causal effect of "not changing the answer."
History-only 13/216 versus near-canonical history inside joint output likewise
motivates an output-contract test; it does not demonstrate that requesting
less information intrinsically reduces a latent memory.

### 10. The Latest Ceiling Limits The Earlier Prose-Deficit Interpretation

The ear ladder's frozen prose full score is 577/2,592. A post-hoc alias
diagnostic finds all revision atoms and active conclusions semantically exact;
the remaining 244 errors are correct `[not_eligible]` followed by `yes`.
Only 55 match a distinct old endpoint. Its typed anchor fails once at 215/216,
so every registered compiler/order/note effect remains **UNIDENTIFIED**.

The new decoder study supplies exact nested schemas and endpoint mapping,
removes the answer from the typed anchor, and reaches 72/72 in typed joint,
prose joint, and staged prose. This reduces the plausibility of an intrinsic
prose-readout limitation on that particular representation. It does not show:

- which change caused recovery: schema, mapping, subset, and schedule differ;
- that staging helps: the single-call prose arm already reaches ceiling;
- that rule computation is solved: active conclusions are explicitly supplied;
- that all 108 earlier cases pass: only 36 were selected, without outcome-based
  selection, from the previously examined surface;
- that malformed intermediate states are handled: no live staged state was wrong;
- that the old gate can be reopened retrospectively.

Perfect calibration is welcome precisely because it removes one source of
ambiguity. It is not a positive effect-size estimate for a favored mechanism.
The two repetitions share 36 cases. A zero-error run is not a general 100%
reliability estimate, and a zero contrast is not an equivalence test.

## Updated Working Model

Keep the motivating hypothesis, but split its commitments:

```text
experimenter-known world Z*
  -> sender computation (latent state unobserved)
  -> task/case/distinction selection under a communication contract
  -> expressed artifact M
  -> reader-indexed state reconstruction
  -> output-field ontology and serialization
  -> endpoint/support decision
  -> utility under a named future query distribution Q

Source re-access, scaffolds, target cues, and repair can act at several arrows.
Post-hoc audits are additional readers, not transparent observations of them.
```

Ontology binding is not just spelling: "active," "suppressed," "conflict,"
and "supported" each admit task-relevant versus plausible alternative meanings.
Serialization is a separate issue: `requires` versus `if` can preserve a rule
while failing a prescribed API shape. Endpoint decoding is separate again.
The arrows are operational distinctions, not an identified internal neural
architecture or a measured multiplicative `B x E x S x R` law.

**Current formulation:** In workflows that reuse expressed artifacts, realized
performance can depend on whether task-relevant distinctions are selected,
made self-contained, reconstructed under the intended ontology, and converted
to a correctly supported decision. Some failures lie in this interface; some
are supplied by an underspecified measurement contract. Improvements can
elicit, supply, stabilize, or redirect computation. Current data do not identify
which of those descriptions applies to every improvement.

This gives an operational quantity `U(M; reader, contract, Q, budget)` and a
load-conditioned reliability surface, not a general linguistic capacity.
"Rate limit" must eventually mean that changing a specified interface improves
downstream work with meaningful alternatives and costs controlled. Merely
finding another prompt-sensitive score is not enough.

| Case/objective binding affects usable messages | Supported locally | Case hints, negative contracts, equal-call controls; self-contract advantage not established |

| More binding always improves all distinctions | Reject as a monotonic working rule | Cue tradeoffs, competing null, adverse typed strong cue |

The local evidence has become more differentiated. The global hypothesis has
not accumulated the same amount of support simply because the archive grew.
"Stagnation" in particular needs a time/training axis that these studies lack.

## Forward Hypothesis: Expression Can Expand What Becomes Thinkable

The original bottleneck formulation invites a diagnostic question: did a
correct state exist and then fail to survive expression? The forward formulation
asks a different, interventional question: **does improving expression change
which distinctions the system can form, organize, and use in subsequent work?**
It does not require assuming that a complete correct state existed beforehand.
The two hypotheses are related, but evidence against an intrinsic deficit in
one codec is not evidence against the second.

"Sender and receiver collusion" is useful shorthand for a possible measurement
blind spot, provided it means compensatory coordination rather than intent.
Potentially different explanations are:

- both use compatible prior conventions, so omitted distinctions need not be
  made explicit for the tested exchange;
- the receiver independently recomputes missing structure;
- the task asks only a coarse endpoint, so different or damaged states receive
  the same score;
- development selects a representation on which the chosen readers work well.

Crossing providers is useful but does not, by itself, eliminate shared
conventions or reader-side reconstruction. Existing source-faithful/repair
contrasts demonstrate reader-indexed outcomes, not a decomposition of these
four explanations. A small final label is not sufficient to measure expressive
range even when communication accuracy is perfect.

The present program has examined **preservation and reuse**, which are parts
of expression. It has not yet examined much of expression as distinction
formation, reframing, or transformation of a sender's or reader's future
capability. Calling the existing work "only transmission" should not erase
its value, but neither should calling it "expression" expand its evidential
scope beyond what was measured.

The forward test should have two separately scored axes:

```text
Expressive change:
  more precise or reusable relations, distinctions, and organization,
  assessed independently of the particular downstream success metric.

Downstream change:
  better reasoning, discrimination, discovery, or generalization on
  reserved tasks not used to choose the expression intervention.
```

Defining expressive ability as "whatever raises the downstream score" would
make the hypothesis circular. Nor does a longer message alone establish an
expressive improvement. Information access, total computation, task exposure,
and evaluation selection must be separated as far as the design allows.

Three complementary paths could test this without reducing expression to
one communication channel:

1. **Sender self-reentry.** Compare an expression-building pass with an
   equal-budget reasoning/control pass, then ask new questions. Improvement
   must exceed merely taking another pass or rereading the source. The existing
   two-pass factorial motivates this but does not isolate expressive growth.
2. **Receiver expansion beyond shared conventions.** Freeze messages before
   revealing held-out queries. Vary reader support and convention familiarity,
   using newly assigned relations or calibrated readers with less reconstructive
   assistance. Include cases where shared-default reconstruction would be
   wrong. Neither humans nor another model family are automatically prior-free.
3. **New-distinction reuse.** Let a sender formulate a distinction without a
   prewritten vocabulary of acceptable concepts; test whether independent
   readers can apply it to held-out observations, discriminate alternatives,
   or solve a newly posed problem. This needs a separate task, not a claim
   that the existing fixed Rule-Z ontology already measures generativity.

For any of these, success supports a bounded claim that a specified expression
intervention expands effective capability. It does not alone establish that
expression is the principal or sole bottleneck of general intelligence.
Failure under one intervention would likewise not refute every possible way
expression might matter. Specify the intervention and prediction before
testing, and retain negative transfer, costs, and competing explanations.

The immediate research priority is therefore to choose **what expressive
change we intend to cause and what independently measured new capability we
predict from it**. A larger perfect transmission score is no longer the main
target. Rule-Z remains the calibration guard for this next question.

The next instrument is the [blind-reading and literary audit pilot](intermediate_audit_pilot.md):
message-only reading, a separate source comparison, independent literary
criticism, and a small human calibration. Its rewrites include source-informed
restoration and intentional omissions, so it is a diagnostic corpus rather
than an isolated aesthetic intervention. Human observations, actual meaning
preservation, and compensatory coordination remain unmeasured at preparation.

A separate [text-boundary and follow-up calibration](text_boundary_calibration.md)
tests whether an initially correct readout survives a false conversational claim,
while retaining the ability to revise an incorrect readout under accurate feedback.
It compares sibling continuations of the same frozen response against neutral
reconsideration, using explicit information-presence controls. This is a proposed
behavioral stability measurement, not evidence that internal confidence is low,
that wording has been optimized, or that sender and receiver coordinate. Its
first transport is serialized-history replay rather than native conversation;
the human reading packet and the original run corpus remain unchanged.

## Avoid An Unfalsifiable Interface Story

An account that calls improvement elicitation, regression competition, and
zero change ceiling can accommodate anything after the fact. To remain useful,
the next bridge commitments must state what would weaken the account:

1. **Contract calibration versus residual encoding.** Under fully explicit
   ontology, matched source information, an answer-free anchor, and fixed
   receiver mapping, a reproducible prose-specific residual would support
   encoding/re-entry work. If it vanishes across reserved structures, retire
   that residual hypothesis for the tested domain instead of moving the label.
2. **Aligned cue benefit versus distractor cost.** Compare contemporaneous
   no-cue, non-imperative padding, generic salience, competing instruction,
   wrong target, and true target. If true target does not exceed the appropriate
   neutral control, withdraw the claim of added target-specific capacity.
3. **Reusable expression versus endpoint compression.** A contract-trained
   message must help independent readers on queries not used to select the
   message. Gains confined to the advertised answer count as task-specific
   compression, not broader expressive capacity.
4. **Interface training versus more computation.** Match useful compute and
   contrast with reasoning-only/process controls. Require held-out rule graphs,
   terminology, readers, and at least one different relation family. If gains
   are only schema compliance, label them that way.
5. **Generativity beyond preservation.** In a separate non-Rule-Z task, a new
   distinction should enable an independent reader's later discrimination on
   held-out observations. Without that result, do not claim that the open
   expressive-generativity hypothesis has been tested.

These are proposed discriminations, not retroactive preregistered tests.

## Next Research Work, Before More Calls

First, human-check a small stratified packet from the new field diagnostics:
catalog versus fired-active, retirement versus suppression, same-conclusion
priority, exact-state/wrong-endpoint, and correct endpoint with incomplete state.
Add counterexamples that look superficially similar but are correct. The
current packet selections are descriptive, not gold labels or training targets.

Second, write one explicit ontology contract and a coverage table of which
old results it would and would not address. The decoder fixed atom schemas and
endpoint mapping; it did **not** calibrate full derivation or source-support
semantics. A small prospective diagnostic is more informative than immediately
repeating the entire prose factorial.

Third, reserve structural and query holdouts before optimizing further. The
seed-29 and seed-101 surfaces are now development/calibration sets. Hash-based
holdouts alone are insufficient when renaming preserves the same problem.

For eventual learning, preserve the original horizon from
[v0](expression_tomography_v0.md#learning-horizon): process traces, rejected
phrases, intended distinctions, collateral meanings, and independent reader
outcomes. Keep source content loss, ontology mismatch, serialization mismatch,
support judgment, and endpoint inconsistency as distinct labels. Do not train
on post-hoc alias acceptance as though it were new generated evidence, and do
not include private-world answers for insufficient sources as successful repairs.

Metaphor currently has one fixture and one receiver call per provider. Its
intended/collateral scores are plumbing smoke evidence, not a replication of
Rule-Z mechanisms, a stylistic ranking, or a measured learning trajectory.
Reader-state transformation and expressive generativity remain open research
spaces. They should not be squeezed into a closed-world oracle merely to
make the narrative tidy.

## Bottom Line

The satisfying part of the latest result is that the instrument can now pass
a previously ambiguous boundary under a clear contract. The more consequential
all-run lesson is that **what looks like lost meaning may instead be missing
case binding, a different field ontology, reader-supplied repair, or an
underbound decision; and exact meaning can still fail to become a supported
action**. The original hypothesis survives as a research question, now with
more specific commitments and several explanations we should stop claiming.
