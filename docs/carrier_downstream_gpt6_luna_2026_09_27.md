# Carrier B1: GPT-6 Luna Downstream Readout

## Result And Scope

Completed **18 GPT-5.6 Luna / low rewrites + 108 GPT-6 Luna / low reads** with
the existing key, no retry, replacement, extra completion or extension. Every
reader output is schema-valid. These are requested models/settings; the adapter
does not retain provider-reported model identity or token billing.

**The known rule-order payload survives literal prose and later literal
readout, but no downstream answer sensitivity is observed in this pilot.**
This separates transmission of extra information from observable use of that
information as an answer code. It does not establish that the model never uses
it internally, that every other carrier is absent, or that the broad
expression/intelligence hypothesis is false.

## What The Comparisons Show

All 108 planned payload comparisons have no current or counterfactual answer
change. The preregistered semantics-preserving stratum contains **84 pairs**,
with **0/84 changes** for either answer. Twenty-four comparisons are excluded
from that stratum because their source messages already assert different
counterfactual answers; they remain in the overall and confounded results.

Across channels, both answers are unchanged in 108/108 comparisons, including
36/36 exact-original versus sorted-rule comparisons. Identical-input repeats
also have 0/54 answer changes. These pairs share observations and only span
three policy families. Zero observed differences is neither an equivalence
test nor proof of a zero population effect. The reader is explicitly asked to
ignore reported answers during recomputation and to treat rule order as
non-priority; this is a calibrated, instructed reader, not arbitrary deployment.

The programmed positive-control reader deliberately follows the known payload
and the same instrumentation detects all 28/28 eligible original-channel
payload changes, while its sorted-channel answers do not change. This is an
offline instrument check, not another model result.

## Two Useful Witnesses

**Contradictory source claims are retained and separately recomputed.** The two
source counterfactual errors produce 12 downstream observations: three channels
x two repetitions per source. In all 12, the literal field retains the original
wrong claim while the recomputed field gives the public-rule answer. This is
not silent repair, because both assertions remain visible side by side.
See the [12 source-contradiction packets](../assets/analyses/carrier_downstream_readout_2026_09_27/source_contradiction_packets.json).

**Decoder abstention does not mean the information disappeared.** The frozen
prose decoder recovers 10/18 input payloads and abstains on eight multi-rule
sentences. Manual source comparison, completed before reader outputs, sees the
original definition order in all eighteen. The later structured readouts recover
the payload in all 36 prose reads, including **16/16 reads of the eight
decoder-abstention sources**. This is a coverage limitation of the conservative
decoder, not evidence that a code was destroyed and reinvented. No decoder was
broadened after observing these outputs.
See the [16 decoder-abstention packets](../assets/analyses/carrier_downstream_readout_2026_09_27/decoder_abstention_packets.json).

## A Remaining Order Channel

A post-run inspection adds an important limitation without changing any score.
Among the sorted channel's 28 eligible payload pairs, **18 have byte-identical
inputs, while 10 still differ in `active_rules` list order**. Sorting only the
rule definitions therefore removes this registered rule-array code, not every
possible surface carrier. We did not fit a new decoder to the active-rule
order or claim that the reader used it. Redundant or alternative channels
remain an explanation that this intervention cannot rule out.
The [secondary order audit](../assets/analyses/carrier_downstream_secondary_order_2026_09_27/analysis.json)
preserves all [paired messages](../assets/analyses/carrier_downstream_secondary_order_2026_09_27/paired_messages.json).
