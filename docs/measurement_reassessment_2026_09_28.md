# Measurement Reassessment After The PG Letters Pilot

## Status And Main Revision

This is a **zero-new-model-call, retrospective interpretation**, not a new
benchmark or a replacement scorer. Read the
[success/failure text contrasts](transmission_success_failure_contrasts_2026_09_28.md)
alongside it. All frozen scores, failed gates, raw messages and previous notes
remain intact. New semantic classifications are source-aware assistant judgments.

The strongest update is not "transmission was an illusion" or "collusion has
been found." It is:

> Success on the agreed question can coexist with failure to preserve the
> distinctions that would make the message reusable outside that question.
> A reader may compensate for an omission, or faithfully propagate an alteration.
> Neither behavior is measured adequately by a final-answer score alone.

The [September 4 synthesis](all_run_hypothesis_synthesis_2026_09_04.md) already
distinguished utility from faithful preservation. PG extends the concern to
human-origin letters and supplies concrete losses in attribution, restriction,
precision and unsupported specificity. It does not invalidate the earlier
calibration results or establish a general expressive-capacity limit.

## What Was Measured

Let `S` be the source, `M` the message, `R` the reader, `C` the public communication
contract, and `Q` the evaluation questions. Keep at least these axes separate:

| Axis | Reference | What a high result does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source fidelity | Protected distinctions in `S` versus `M` | External historical truth, unstated author intention, or all possible meanings |
| Reading fidelity | What `M` supports versus what `R` reports | Fidelity of `M` to `S` |
| Task utility | Correct responses to the declared `Q` | Preservation of distinctions not tested by `Q` |
| Reusability | New questions/interventions after `M` is frozen | General intelligence or unrestricted transfer |
| Literary effect | Human/critic responses under a separate rubric | Truth, entailment, or information preservation |
| Format compliance | Frozen parser and schema | Reasoning correctness; conversely, parseability is not truth |

For a fixed question set, two sources can be equivalent for scoring:

```text
S1 ~Q S2  iff  every q in Q has the same reference answer on S1 and S2.
```

Answer accuracy cannot distinguish members of this equivalence class. A single
Rule-Z eligibility label is a particularly coarse projection. Even a correct
active-conclusion set can omit which rules produced it or how another fact
would change it. B2 now gives an explicit archived example of this distinction.
Later extraction, state, intervention and carrier studies already enlarged `Q`;
it would be inaccurate to call the entire history label-only evaluation.

If the communication contract asks only for a label, discarding other details
can be legitimate compression. If it asks for reusable rules or the meaning of
a letter, the preservation obligations are larger. **Declare those obligations
before using an omission as an error.** PG's broad preservation instruction and
five provisional questions do not yet define exhaustive semantic gold.

## Defining Sender-Reader Coupling

Use a neutral umbrella, **sender-reader-task coupling**, before the stronger
word *collusion*. These are different mechanisms, not rungs of an inevitable
ladder and not one composite score:

| Mechanism | Operational distinction | Present status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public semantic convention | Shared vocabulary, explicit rules, public answer mapping | Necessary for ordinary communication; not collusion |
| Reader compensation | A reader supplies integration/inference not explicitly stated | Faithful/repair contrasts show permission-sensitive recoverability; not every inferred process is identified |
| Visible error propagation | A changed assertion in `M` appears in the reading despite not being supported by `S` | PG session specificity and attribution are concrete provisional examples; no hidden carrier needed |
| Fidelity-blind evaluation | A protected distinction is wrong or absent while the selected endpoint passes | Directly exhibited by B2's false suppressions and correct labels; this alone is a property of the measurement, not cooperation |
| Nonsemantic cue use | At fixed protected meaning, a surface intervention changes answers | Requires controlled paired tests. Order sensitivity alone does not identify a codebook |
| Covert coded coordination | An undeclared payload is encoded and causally used by a receiver through a shared mapping, outside the agreed public semantic contract | Not identified in these runs. Carrier survival is insufficient; the mapping and downstream use need intervention evidence |

For discussion, **functional collusion** can name the hypothesis that sender and
reader systematically exploit a common shortcut so that high task utility masks
failure of the declared fidelity obligation. Its components must still be
tested: the violation, the shortcut's use, and its effect on apparent success.
Do not infer it merely because both readers agree, prose is formulaic, or a
score misses an error. Do not infer intent, deception, co-training or shared
hidden state. No optimization of this sender-reader pair was performed here.

For the narrower covert-code claim, require at least: a protected-meaning
invariance check; payload/meaning crossing; a verified decoder or mapping;
downstream sensitivity to payload at fixed meaning; and held-out mapping
reversal/erasure controls that discriminate decoding from order difficulty.
Known-positive engineered channels calibrate detection, not natural emergence.
"Encrypted" would additionally require an encryption mechanism; ordinary
compression or a preserved list order does not meet that description.

## What The Judge Papers Contribute

The role of these papers is methodological, not external proof of collusion
in our data. We need to audit the evaluator as well as the message.

- [Reliability without Validity](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.19544v1) separates
  stable judgments, agreement and bias, and recommends position swaps,
  replication and cross-benchmark validation. Its experiments concern judge
  benchmarks; they do not establish our mechanism. Chance correction does not
  by itself validate the construct or the reference. In particular, do not
  replace deterministic Rule-Z oracle accuracy with inter-rater kappa: they
  answer different questions.
- [Limitations of LLM-as-a-Judge in Expert Knowledge Tasks](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.20266v1)
  reports domain- and aspect-dependent expert/judge agreement in dietetics and
  mental health. The transferable lesson is to inspect dimension-specific
  disagreements, not to import its agreement rates into literary fidelity.
- [Explaining Length Bias](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.01085v5) motivates controlling
  length in preference comparisons. This is not a semantic-fidelity estimator.
  The first paper finds small verbosity bias under its tested rubric, so there
  is no universal license to explain every judge error by length.
- [Hidden in Plain Text](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03768) demonstrates learned
  steganographic communication under its training/in-context optimization
  setups and limits of paraphrasing defenses. It motivates testing use after
  rewriting, but is not evidence that our untrained pair adopted such a scheme.
- The requested [v2 framework](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.03768v2) explicitly
  distinguishes task success, payload decoding and overseer bypass, and
  calibrates oversight on honest runs. We carry forward that separation:
  altered prose or an approving critic is not itself a covert-code result.
- [A Survey on LLM-as-a-Judge, v5](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.15594v5) treats
  agreement, bias and adversarial robustness as distinct evaluator properties.
  Its meta-evaluation motivates testing our proposed judge against known edits,
  including benign controls, before using it to label transmission failures.
  A role prompt or multiple judges is not a substitute for that calibration.

The previously supplied ScienceDirect page (`S2666675825004564`) could not be
retrieved in this pass; no new substantive claim here relies on its contents.

Our own audit must obey the same discipline. A critic role is not an independent
measurement guarantee. Source fidelity, reader faithfulness, usefulness and
beauty should not be averaged into one compensatory score. For source-grounded
factual judgments, use known minimal edits as positive controls, benign edits
as negative controls, hidden model/condition labels, balanced presentation order,
and human adjudication of ambiguous claims. Record the message-only reading
before showing the source; blind auditors to downstream success until their
source/message distinctions are recorded. Otherwise the proposed reverse audit
could itself reward the explanation that fits the known outcome.
With one or two familiar humans,
report disagreements and exposure, not a population reliability coefficient.

## Bottleneck Hypothesis Update

| Hypothesis | Current disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Natural language is intrinsically too weak to carry the tested Rule-Z distinctions | Broad version not supported: many controlled and ordinary-prose successes remain |
| Under a given binding, budget and reader contract, generation drops or alters task-relevant distinctions | Supported locally, with multiple causes; missing facts, incomplete output and reader computation must be separated |
| Current-task closure can conceal loss of future-use distinctions | Supported as a measurement concern and by concrete counterexamples; magnitude outside these fixtures is unknown |
| Improving expression expands independently tested reasoning/discovery | Still a positive, testable hypothesis, not an observed result |
| Hidden coded coordination explains the apparent successes | Open, with no identified code-use mechanism; ordinary public derivation is a strong competing explanation |

The useful positive version is: **increasing the controllable preservation and
reuse of distinctions may expand what a system can subsequently reason about.**
This includes selecting what matters, preserving relations and uncertainty,
and making them accessible to new readers and questions. It is not simply
longer prose, extra labels, or prettier wording.

PG was not forced into a tight compression budget; its sender voluntarily
shortened all three excerpts. Default summarization, task interpretation and
selection errors are live explanations. A rate/capacity bottleneck requires a
budget-performance frontier and interventions, not one shortened sample.
Human-origin texts also do not guarantee absence from model pretraining.

## Next Discriminating Tests, Not Yet Run

1. **Reverse-audit both successes and failures.** Use the companion contrast
   packet, then freeze an outcome-stratified sample of additional successes.
   Annotate actual statements, missing distinctions, support, derived-state
   availability and endpoint agreement separately. Do not select only dramatic
   failures and call the proportion a corpus rate.
2. **Repair one meaning-bearing clause at a time.** For the frozen PG messages,
   compare the original relay, minimal restoration, a similarly sized neutral
   edit and an identity-source baseline. Freeze fresh attribution, exclusion
   and time probes before calls. Verify source ambiguity with a human first.
   Keep the original five-question results unchanged.
3. **Separate closure from reuse.** Freeze messages before selecting a held-out
   query battery. Include inactive rules, changed goals, negation scope,
   commitments, uncertainty and speaker attribution. Compare label-only,
   active-conclusion-only, full public rules and ordinary prose as explicit
   sufficiency controls. Their losses differ by contract, not by typography.
4. **Keep covert-use calibration distinct.** Cross equivalent content and known
   payload, then reverse the mapping on held-out variants. Use verified benign
   surface edits to distinguish a code from processing sensitivity. Do not let
   a PG fidelity repair count as a steganography test.
5. **Only then test the positive intelligence claim.** Match accessible evidence,
   call count and recorded budgets; track length separately. Test whether an
   expression intervention improves new reasoning, not just the practiced
   readout. A later training study needs held-out structures and its own explicit
   approval; this analysis authorizes neither training nor API calls.
