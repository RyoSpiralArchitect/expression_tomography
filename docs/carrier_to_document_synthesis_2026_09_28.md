# From Carrier Tests To Human-Origin Documents

## Working Conclusion

The current evidence supports a measurement decomposition, not a verdict on
hidden communication or the general expression/intelligence bottleneck:

```text
Source distinctions
  -> what the sender binds as the communication target
  -> distinctions and other cues present in the message
  -> what the reader reconstructs
  -> how the reader applies and updates those distinctions
  -> the response's serialization
  -> what the evaluator accepts and scores
```

Literal fidelity, carrier recoverability, reader use, reported structure and
output-contract compliance must not be treated as interchangeable successes.
This extends the [earlier all-run synthesis](all_run_hypothesis_synthesis_2026_09_04.md)
with the A/B1/B2/B3 sequence. It is a descriptive synthesis of inspected cases,
not a newly preregistered theory or a new model run.

## Four Updates

### Carrier Survival Is A Real Observation, Not A Use Verdict

Order payloads survive structured readout and prose rewriting. That keeps the
hidden-feature concern live: paraphrasing is not automatically decontamination.
However, GPT's B1 answers do not vary across the eligible payload contrasts,
while Mistral also varies under identical inputs. Two payload-following Mistral
contrasts repeat one source pair. Ordinary order sensitivity and inconsistency
remain explanations; no codebook reversal or intentional coordination was shown.

Shared conventions and reader compensation can also be legitimate communication.
Do not label all compression, inference or cross-model agreement as collusion.
The operational target is whether a non-semantic feature causally controls a
downstream distinction after relevant semantic content has been held fixed.
The present record does not identify that stronger effect.

### Preserved Fields Do Not Guarantee Consistent Use

The [offline mechanism audit](carrier_reader_mechanism_audit_2026_09_27.md) finds
75/90 Mistral current/future disagreements where the public conclusion set is
unchanged, versus zero for GPT. These are dependent observations and overlapping
invariance checks, not 90 independent worlds. Candidate mistakes overlap and
leave all eighteen f08.w0 errors unexplained; the catalogue is not an inferred
internal program.

B2 removes source answer claims and the known rule-definition order carrier.
Errors remain. Thus those features are not necessary for these failures; this
does not remove every possible cue. The base-only Mistral reader returns the
right current active set in 12/12 without being given that set, yet still
mislabels both f02.w1 repetitions. Copying an explicitly supplied active field
cannot explain those new readouts, but their production process remains hidden.

### More Structure Is Not A Monotone Repair

Five of Mistral's six correct B2 future labels accompany an incorrect reported
trace. Materialization and trace achieve the same 6/12 total on different cases;
requesting trace fields also lowers current-label correctness from 10/12 to
6/12. Reported facts remain right. Intermediate output is an intervention, not a
window onto the previous response's internal reasoning.

The frozen B2 output fields were lexicographically ordered: the answer appears
before the facts and firing fields. That is a documented interface limitation,
not an established causal explanation. B3 removes multi-field output order by
asking for one boolean, but also changes inputs and the requested operation.
It therefore cannot isolate an output-order effect.

### Serialization And Content Need Separate Endpoints

All 64 Mistral B3 responses are fenced JSON. The same convention occurs in all
36 Mistral B2 responses, where the lenient parser accepts it. B3's strict-parser
failure is real under its declared contract, but not a new collapse of logic.

The secondary audit removes only a complete outer fence and preserves all inner
content. It was defined after raw-response inspection and is explicitly post
hoc. It yields 16/16 firing, 13/16 suppression, 16/16 set membership and 16/16
label verification for Mistral, while primary scores remain unchanged.

The three errors concern two suppression probes: one changes answer on an
identical-prompt repetition; the reversed-priority probe fails twice. This
preserves a narrow priority-application concern, not a general mechanism label.
Local verification success is evidence about that scaffolded task only.

## Updated Research Question

The forward hypothesis remains useful in a more discriminating form:

> Can improving how a model binds and expresses task-relevant distinctions
> improve independently measured downstream reasoning, beyond answer cues,
> shared conventions, extra compute or an externally supplied computation?

Current runs establish neither the general gain nor its absence. A future
causal test needs frozen meaning targets, independent readers, budget controls
and interventions whose semantic effects are audited. The original message,
reader reconstruction, use/update and output format need separate measures.
An end-to-end score alone can hide compensation or a correct endpoint with
wrong intermediate distinctions.

Training remains prospective. Before optimizing a sender against receiver or
judge scores, establish that improvements preserve independently grounded
distinctions instead of specializing to one evaluator or making a better code.
The present PR neither starts training nor adds a new live-call budget.

## Why Human-Origin Text Comes Next

The [next protocol](natural_document_transmission_plan_2026_09_28.md) starts with
independently authored letters and news/reportage, not LLM-generated cases.
That removes one source of task-specific source construction. It does not
guarantee unfamiliarity to models, eliminate priors, or prevent the first LLM
rewrite from developing a new shorthand.

The next checkpoint is **source selection**, not another immediate model run:

1. Select six coherent passages in one original language, three letters and
   three news/reportage items, with verifiable authorship and reuse boundaries.
2. Record edition, exact locator, transcription/OCR uncertainty, relevant omitted
   context, and possible prior familiarity. Do not silently substitute an LLM
   translation or summary for the original.
3. Prepare a private, human-reviewed evidence ledger that distinguishes explicit
   assertions, supported inferences and unresolved ambiguity. Non-mention is not
   falsehood in an open natural document.
4. Keep original-direct, one-hop and two-hop readings separate. Freeze questions
   before generation but keep them hidden from senders. Give naive human readers
   a message-first allocation that does not expose the original or answer key.
5. Predeclare output compliance and any exact wrapper normalization separately.
   Preserve source-aware fidelity audit, reader recovery and literary preference
   as different readouts, rather than one reward.

This direction can reveal losses in attribution, modality, temporal relations,
reference, implication, ambiguity and voice that a Rule-Z answer label cannot
cover. That is a prospective measurement opportunity, not an observed result.

## Review Surface

Review the linked B2 and B3 notes before the large generated evidence trees.
The implementation lives in the two new sibling task packages; shared provider
code and earlier frozen experiments are not rewritten. Important checks are:

- Source snapshots and every raw journal/SQLite asset must be committed, not
  merely present in the author's working directory.
- Reanalysis must make zero model calls, preserve primary scores and retain
  invalid/missing denominators.
- Post-hoc wrapper results must never be presented as the preregistered endpoint.
- Pair and repetition counts must not become independent-sample statistics.
- No observation should be promoted to hidden reasoning, isolated family bias,
  general expression gain, literary quality or a collusion verdict.

The automated tests exercise those boundaries and reconstruct the live bundles.
Code review and CI do not constitute independent human validation of the
scientific interpretation or future natural-document labels.
